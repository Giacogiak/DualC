the explorer (`dualc-explorer.html`) draws its own. They are assets, not pages: outside the
doc-size contract like everything under `docs/raw/`, and never edited (the rule is in
[`docs/raw/README.md`](../../README.md)). `fig-tpms.svg` at 655 KB is the one large file — 4,400
`<line>` and 1,291 `<rect>` elements, a rendered lattice rather than a drawing; it is the reason
the pack is 1.7 MB on disk while its text is 0.6 MB.
